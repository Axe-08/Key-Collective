## 3. Break-Even Analysis: Community Pool Value vs. Infrastructure Cost

### Value Generated by Communal Pool at 1M Requests/Day

- **Infrastructure cost:** ~$16.50/mo
- **Equivalent commercial gateway cost** (1M reqs/day at OpenRouter free tier): Hard-capped at ~50 RPD equivalent → impossible without paying
- **Equivalent at Requesty:** $0.002/req × 30M reqs = **$60,000/mo** (with 5% markup)
- **Equivalent self-hosted LiteLLM:** VPS + Redis + Postgres = **$50-100/mo** idle, plus compute overages

### Community Pool Economic Efficiency Multiplier

$$\text{Value Ratio} = \frac{\text{Commercial equivalent cost}}{\text{Infrastructure cost}} = \frac{\$60{,}000}{\$16.50} \approx 3{,}600\times$$

The community pool delivers **3,600× more economic value** than its infrastructure cost at 1M requests/day.

## 5. Cost Optimization Recommendations

1. **Cache D1 auth lookups in KeyPoolDO** — eliminates 80% of D1 reads; estimated savings: $0.50/mo at 1M req/day
2. **Batch WAE writes** via `ctx.waitUntil()` — already non-blocking; no further optimization needed
3. **PoolCoordinatorDO flush interval** — 30s default is optimal (balances consistency vs. D1 IOPS)
4. **DO alarm for hot-path warming** — prevents cold-start latency spikes; no cost overhead
